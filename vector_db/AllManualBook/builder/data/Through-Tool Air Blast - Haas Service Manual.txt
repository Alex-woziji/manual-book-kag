## Spring & Ball

 TAB Air Check Valve Inspection

 Electrical Schematic


into the valve, the ball gets pushed
back down due to both air and
gravity [4].

The new shuttle and ball check
valve also has a new elbow fitting
that is slotted [1]. This elbow fitting
allows a small amount of coolant or
air pressure through to pressurize
the pressure sensor.

Coolant pressure can push the ball
into the spring and trap it, causing
the check valve to fail. Without a
functional check valve, air from the
TAB may flow backward through
the coolant system and cause the
coolant tank to overflow and/or
bubble.

Corrective Action:

Disassemble and visually inspect
the check valve for any deformities.
Make sure the ball isn't trapped
inside the spring. If it is, the spring
would be pushing the ball against
the countersink of the fitting.

When the TAB air check valve [1] fails
and TSC is turned on, coolant from the
TSC pump will back flow through the
TAB air line to the solenoid and blow
out the muffler.

CORRECTIVE ACTION:

Replace the TAB air check valve.
